Our Story

Made with Heart,
Not Shortcuts

Emily Kitchen started as a small passion project — rolling mochi at midnight, experimenting with textures and fillings until each bite felt like a moment of quiet joy.

Today, every mochi is still made by hand, one at a time, using the same care and the same philosophy: never compromise on ingredients, never rush the process.

🌾
Premium Ingredients Only Authentic Japanese rice flour, fresh fruits, and premium chocolate.
🤲
100% Handcrafted Daily No machines, no freezers. Made fresh in small batches each morning.
✨
The Perfect Chewy Texture Soft yet springy, never gummy — our daily obsession and our promise.
